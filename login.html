How Login Works

When you sign in with Google:

  • You authorize our app to access specific data based on the requested scopes.
  • Google provides us with an *OAuth access token*, which is securely sent to our backend.
  • Our server verifies the token with Google to authenticate you.
  • We use the granted scopes to perform necessary tasks (e.g., accessing your profile or email).
